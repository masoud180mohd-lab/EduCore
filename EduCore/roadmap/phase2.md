# Roadmap Phase 2: Growth And Operational Depth

## Timeline

Months 7-12.

## Goal

Strengthen product depth, communication, reporting, and operational reliability.

## Scope

- WhatsApp notifications.
- Notification preferences and consent.
- Full reconciliation workflow.
- Finance reports.
- Improved report cards.
- Exam moderation.
- Parent engagement dashboard.
- Library basic module.
- Inventory basic module.
- Headmaster dashboard.
- Offline marks workflow after attendance is proven.
- Search adapter if needed.
- AI low-risk features.
- Feature flags.
- Better SLO dashboards.

## AI Scope

Allowed:

- Report comment drafts.
- Admin summaries.
- Dashboard explanations.

Not yet:

- Automated high-stakes student prediction without evaluation.
- Teacher performance scoring as judgement.

## Success Metrics

- 50-100 schools supported.
- Notification delivery tracked.
- Payment exceptions handled in product.
- Offline marks sync has controlled conflict flow.
- AI costs visible per tenant.
- Production incidents have runbooks.

## Critical Engineering Gates

- Outbox events mature.
- Notification delivery ledger.
- AI Gateway in production.
- Access review workflow.
- Restore drill repeated.
