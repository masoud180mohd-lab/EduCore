# Roadmap Phase 3: Scale And Platform Expansion

## Timeline

Months 13-24.

## Goal

Scale the platform, introduce carefully governed AI predictions, and prepare ecosystem capabilities.

## Scope

- AI early warning system after evaluation.
- Fee default prediction after finance data quality is proven.
- Government reporting exports.
- Transport module.
- Hostel module.
- HR/payroll module.
- Advanced analytics.
- API foundation for approved partners.
- White-label support.
- Service extraction where justified.
- Read replicas and scaling improvements.
- Stronger DR targets.

## Possible Service Extractions

- Notification service.
- Payment orchestration.
- AI Gateway.
- Report generation.
- Search indexing.
- Analytics pipeline.

## Success Metrics

- 500+ schools supported.
- p95 latency within target for common workflows.
- Payment reconciliation SLA defined and met.
- AI predictions evaluated and human-reviewed.
- No critical privacy incidents.
- DR target improved.

## Critical Engineering Gates

- Sharding decision based on metrics, not guesses.
- Enterprise access controls.
- Data retention automation.
- Advanced audit reporting.
- Partner API scopes and consent model.
