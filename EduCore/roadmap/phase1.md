# Roadmap Phase 1: MVP Foundation

## Timeline

Months 1-6.

## Goal

Launch a trustworthy pilot-ready platform for core school operations.

## Scope

- Tenant onboarding.
- Identity and roles.
- Student registry.
- Academic structure.
- Teacher attendance.
- Basic offline attendance.
- Marks entry and approval.
- Basic report cards.
- Fee structures.
- Invoices.
- M-Pesa or one primary payment provider.
- Receipts.
- Payment reconciliation basics.
- SMS notifications.
- Parent portal basics.
- Observability foundation.
- Backups and restore drill.

## Excluded

- Full WhatsApp automation.
- Full AI prediction.
- Transport GPS.
- Hostel.
- Advanced HR/payroll.
- Blockchain certificates.
- Marketplace APIs.

## Success Metrics

- 3-5 pilot schools onboarded.
- Attendance captured daily.
- Payments reconciled with no unresolved critical mismatch.
- Parent portal used by pilot parents.
- No tenant isolation incidents.
- Restore drill completed.

## Critical Engineering Gates

- RLS implemented.
- Permission tests implemented.
- Ledger invariants implemented.
- Offline attendance sync tested.
- Payment webhook replay tested.
- Audit events for sensitive actions.
