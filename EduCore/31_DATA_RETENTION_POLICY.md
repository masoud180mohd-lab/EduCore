# 31 Data Retention Policy

## Purpose

Define how long EduCore keeps data and how deletion or archiving should work.

## Default Retention

- Financial records: 7 years.
- Receipts: 7 years.
- Audit events: 7 years for sensitive actions.
- Login history: 1-2 years depending on policy.
- Notification delivery logs: 1-2 years, content shorter where possible.
- Academic records: configurable, often long-term.
- Health and discipline records: strict retention by school/legal policy.

## Deletion Principles

- Do not hard delete financial records.
- Use reversals for finance corrections.
- Soft delete operational records where recovery is needed.
- Delete or anonymize data when retention expires and law allows.

## Backups

Backup retention must align with privacy and recovery requirements. Deletion from active systems does not instantly remove data from backups; policy must explain this.

## Tenant Exit

Tenant exit process should include:

- export.
- billing closure.
- data retention window.
- deletion/anonymization schedule.
- legal hold exceptions.
