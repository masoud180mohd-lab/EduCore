# 17 Privacy And Data Governance

## Privacy Position

EduCore stores child data, academic records, financial data, health notes, discipline records, and parent communication. Privacy is a product requirement, not a legal afterthought.

## Data Classification

Classify every field:

- Public.
- Internal.
- Sensitive.
- Child-sensitive.
- Health-sensitive.
- Financial.
- Credential-like.
- Audit-critical.

## Consent

Consent is required for:

- parent communication preferences.
- AI features involving student-specific insight where required.
- partner data sharing.
- future ecosystem APIs.

## Data Minimization

Collect only data needed for clear product purpose. Do not collect national IDs, medical details, or behavioral data without explicit requirement and protection.

## Access

Access to private data must be:

- role-limited.
- relationship-limited.
- logged when sensitive.
- reviewable.
- revocable.

## AI Privacy

AI calls must minimize context and redact PII where possible. No student data should train external models unless contracts and laws allow it.

## Data Subject Rights

Plan for:

- export.
- correction.
- deletion where legally allowed.
- retention exceptions.
- audit of access.

## Data Sharing

Government, bank, university, employer, and partner sharing must use explicit scopes and audit trails.
