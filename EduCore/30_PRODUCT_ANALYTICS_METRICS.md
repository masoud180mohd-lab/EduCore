# 30 Product Analytics And Metrics

## Purpose

Measure product value, reliability, and business health without violating privacy.

## Product Metrics

- active schools.
- active teachers.
- active parents.
- attendance sessions completed.
- marks submitted.
- invoices issued.
- payments completed.
- report cards generated.
- notifications delivered.

## Adoption Metrics

- weekly active schools.
- teacher attendance usage.
- parent portal activation.
- payment method adoption.
- offline sync usage.

## Reliability Metrics

- payment confirmation delay.
- sync delay.
- notification delivery delay.
- login success rate.
- dashboard latency.

## Business Metrics

- MRR.
- gross margin by tenant.
- SMS/WhatsApp cost.
- AI cost.
- churn risk.
- support tickets per tenant.

## Privacy Rules

Analytics events should not include raw child-sensitive data, health notes, payment secrets, or full message bodies.
