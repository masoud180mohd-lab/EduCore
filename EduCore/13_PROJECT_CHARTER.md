# 13 Project Charter

## Purpose

EduCore exists to become the trusted operating platform for school operations in Africa. It must help schools run academics, finance, communication, and insights with reliability even in weak internet environments.

## Mandate

Build a SaaS platform that schools can trust for daily operations and long-term records.

## Success Definition

The project succeeds when pilot schools can:

- onboard without engineering assistance.
- register students and staff.
- capture attendance reliably.
- process fees and receipts without disputes.
- communicate with parents.
- generate academic reports.
- recover from operational failures.

## Strategic Boundaries

EduCore will not chase every school feature at once. It will first win trust in core workflows, then expand into the wider education ecosystem.

## Stakeholders

- Students.
- Parents.
- Teachers.
- School leadership.
- Finance staff.
- School owners.
- Government education officers.
- Platform operators.
- Payment and communication partners.

## Governance

Major changes require an ADR when they affect:

- architecture.
- tenancy.
- security.
- payments.
- ledger.
- AI.
- offline sync.
- data retention.
- deployment model.

## Delivery Standard

No feature is complete unless it is safe, observable, documented, tested, and recoverable.
