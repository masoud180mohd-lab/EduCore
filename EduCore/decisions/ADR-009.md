# ADR-009: Low-Risk AI First

## Status

Accepted

## Context

EduCore's AI vision includes predictions, recommendations, voice, tutoring, and summaries. Some AI features affect children, teachers, and finances.

## Decision

EduCore will launch low-risk AI first: drafts, summaries, explanations, and help. High-risk AI predictions require evaluation, consent, human review, and governance.

## Consequences

Benefits:

- Faster safe AI delivery.
- Lower reputational risk.
- Lower compliance risk.
- Better data quality before predictions.

Trade-offs:

- Some impressive AI features are delayed.

## Rules

- No high-stakes automated decisions.
- AI Gateway required.
- Evaluation required for predictions.
- Human review required for sensitive outputs.
