# ADR-004: Ledger Is Source Of Truth

## Status

Accepted

## Context

EduCore handles school fees, receipts, payment reconciliation, discounts, scholarships, refunds, and financial reports. Directly updating fee balances can create disputes and hidden accounting errors.

## Decision

The finance ledger is the source of truth. Balances must be derived from immutable journal entries and allocations.

## Consequences

Benefits:

- Better auditability.
- Safer corrections.
- Reliable financial reports.
- Stronger school trust.

Trade-offs:

- More complex implementation.
- Requires accounting discipline.
- Reports may need projections for performance.

## Rules

- Posted journal entries are immutable.
- Corrections use reversals.
- Every journal entry balances.
- Payment callbacks never directly update balances without ledger posting.
