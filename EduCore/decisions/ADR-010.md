# ADR-010: Workflow-Level Observability

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Server uptime alone does not prove the platform works. Schools care whether payments confirm, attendance syncs, and messages arrive.

## Decision

EduCore observability must measure business workflows as first-class signals.

## Consequences

Benefits:

- Faster incident detection.
- Better support.
- Better reliability.
- Clearer business health.

Trade-offs:

- Requires instrumentation discipline.
- More metrics to govern.

## Rules

- Every request/job has correlation ID.
- Critical workflows have SLOs.
- Payment, notification, sync, and AI dashboards are required.
