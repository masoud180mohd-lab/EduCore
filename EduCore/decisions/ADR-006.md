# ADR-006: Relationship-Aware Authorization

## Status

Accepted

## Context

School access is relational. Parents access their children. Teachers access assigned classes or subjects. Government officers may have limited temporary access.

## Decision

EduCore authorization will combine RBAC, relationship-based access, ABAC, explicit deny rules, delegation, and RLS.

## Consequences

Benefits:

- Safer child data protection.
- More accurate school workflows.
- Better support for complex roles.

Trade-offs:

- More complex policy testing.
- Requires policy explanation tooling over time.

## Rules

- Do not rely on role alone.
- Maintain golden authorization tests.
- Support access must be time-bound and audited.
- Explicit deny can override broad role grants.
